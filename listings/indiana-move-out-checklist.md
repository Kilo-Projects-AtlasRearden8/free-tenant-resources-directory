# Indiana Move-Out Checklist & Security Deposit

**Category:** Move-in/move-out checklists  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** Indiana Legal Services  
**Official link:** https://www.indianalegalservices.org

## Steps

1. Review your lease move-out and notice requirements.
2. Clean the unit and remove all personal belongings.
3. Complete a final walkthrough with the landlord if possible.
4. Take dated photos of the empty, cleaned unit.
5. Provide your forwarding address in writing for the deposit return.
