# USA.gov Tenant Rights

**Category:** Lease checklists  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** USA.gov  
**Official link:** https://www.usa.gov/tenant-rights

## Steps

1. Visit USA.gov/tenant-rights for the federal renter overview.
2. Review your lease terms and state security-deposit rules.
3. Find your state or local tenant-rights office through the directory.
4. Document repair requests and keep dated copies.
5. Contact a local legal-aid office if your landlord violates the lease.

## About

Federal USA.gov guide to tenant rights, leases, security deposits, and where to find local help.
