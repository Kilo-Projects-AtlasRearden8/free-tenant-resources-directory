# Repair Request Letter Template

**Category:** Repair-request letters  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** Indiana Legal Services  
**Official link:** https://www.indianalegalservices.org

## Steps

1. Document the issue with photos and dates.
2. Write a dated repair-request letter describing the problem clearly.
3. Reference your lease clause requiring landlord maintenance.
4. Keep a copy and send it by certified mail or hand-deliver with a receipt.
5. Allow a reasonable time for repairs, then follow up in writing.

## About

Template for notifying your landlord in writing about needed repairs, with steps to document and follow up.
