# Indiana Tenant Rights Handbook

**Category:** Lease checklists  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** Indiana Legal Services  
**Official link:** https://www.indianalegalservices.org

## Steps

1. Review the Indiana Tenant Rights Handbook for your county.
2. Check your lease for illegal clauses before signing.
3. Document the unit's condition with photos at move-in.
4. Know the Indiana eviction notice timeline and your response window.
5. Keep a paper trail of rent payments and landlord communications.

## About

An Indiana-specific guide to tenant rights, leases, eviction process, and security deposits from Indiana Legal Services.
