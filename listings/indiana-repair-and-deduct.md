# Indiana Repair-and-Deduct Notice Guide

**Category:** Repair-request letters  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** Indiana Legal Services  
**Official link:** https://www.indianalegalservices.org

## Steps

1. Confirm the repair affects health, safety, or habitability.
2. Notify the landlord in writing and keep proof of delivery.
3. Review Indiana Legal Services repair-and-deduct rules for your situation.
4. Document all repair costs with receipts before deducting.
5. Contact Indiana Legal Services before withholding or deducting rent.

## About

Indiana Legal Services guidance on repair-and-deduct options when a landlord fails to make essential repairs.
