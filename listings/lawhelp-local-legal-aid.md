# Find Free Local Legal Aid

**Category:** Repair-request letters  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** LawHelp.org  
**Official link:** https://www.lawhelp.org

## Steps

1. Go to LawHelp.org and select your state.
2. Search for housing or tenant-rights legal aid programs.
3. Check income eligibility for free services.
4. Gather your lease, notices, and repair documentation.
5. Contact the legal aid office to request assistance.

## About

LawHelp.org directory to locate free civil legal aid and tenant-rights assistance by state.
