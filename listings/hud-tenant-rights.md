# HUD Tenant Rights & Lease Basics

**Category:** Lease checklists  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)  
**Official link:** https://www.hud.gov/topics/rental_assistance

## Steps

1. Learn your federal fair-housing rights before signing anything.
2. Read the full lease carefully; never sign a blank form.
3. Confirm who is responsible for repairs and maintenance.
4. Check security-deposit limits and return rules for your state.
5. Get every agreement and landlord promise in writing.
6. Keep copies of the lease, move-in checklist, and all notes.

## About

This HUD page is the federal starting point for tenant rights, leases, security deposits, and fair housing. Use it alongside your state and local renter resources.
