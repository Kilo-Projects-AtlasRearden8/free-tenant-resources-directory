# Responding to an Indiana Eviction Notice

**Category:** Lease checklists  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** Indiana Legal Services  
**Official link:** https://www.indianalegalservices.org

## Steps

1. Read the eviction notice carefully and note the deadline.
2. Do not ignore the notice; respond within the time window.
3. Contact Indiana Legal Services or a local legal-aid office immediately.
4. Gather your lease, payment records, and landlord communications.
5. Appear for any scheduled court date if one is set.

Indiana Legal Services guidance on how to respond when you receive an eviction notice.
