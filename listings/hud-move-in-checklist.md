# HUD Move-In Condition Checklist

**Category:** Move-in/move-out checklists  
**Cost:** Free ($0)  
**Source:** U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)  
**Official link:** https://www.hud.gov/topics/rental_assistance

## Steps

1. Complete the checklist with your landlord before moving in.
2. Walk through every room and note existing damage.
3. Photograph walls, floors, appliances, and fixtures.
4. Have both you and the landlord sign and date the checklist.
5. Keep a signed copy for your records.

## About

HUD move-in checklist to record a rental unit's condition and protect your security deposit.
